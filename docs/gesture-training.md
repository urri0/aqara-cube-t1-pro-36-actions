# Gesture training

Use a hard surface.

```text
shake  = sharp up/down shake
throw  = short sharp downward jerk in hand
slide  = slow short slide on a hard surface, only 2–3 cm
rotate = slow controlled rotation on table
tap    = two light taps with the cube on the table
```

Soft surfaces like beds, sofas and carpets make slide, tap and rotate unreliable.

Do not tap the top of the cube with your finger. Tap means tapping the cube itself on the table twice.

Do not drag slide 10–15 cm. A short controlled 2–3 cm slide is enough.
